Writing Tips:

  • Start with "This summer I..." or "My favorite summer activity is..."
  • Include details about what you did and how you felt
  • Write about people you spent time with
  • Describe places you went and things you saw
  • Share what you learned or discovered
  • Tell about your favorite summer foods and treats
  • End with what you're excited about or looking forward to
