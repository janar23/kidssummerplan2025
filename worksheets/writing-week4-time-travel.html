⏰ Write About Time Travel ⏰

Imagine traveling through time and space!

Your Mission:

Write a story about time travel! You can travel to the past, future, or both. Think about what you would see, who you would meet, and what adventures you would have.

🚀 Time Travel Ideas to Get You Started:

🦕 Travel to the Past:

  • Visit the dinosaurs and see what they were really like
  • Go back to when your parents were kids
  • See how people lived 100 years ago
  • Meet famous people from history
  • See how your town looked long ago

🚀 Travel to the Future:

  • See flying cars and robot helpers
  • Visit space colonies on Mars
  • Meet your future self
  • See how technology has changed
  • Find out what school is like in the future

🎭 Time Travel Adventures:

  • Fix something that went wrong in the past
  • Bring back a pet from the past
  • Learn secrets from the future
  • Meet your great-great-grandparents
  • See what your house looked like when it was built

💡 Helpful Words for Your Time Travel Story:

time machine portal past future present adventure journey discover explore amazing strange different surprised excited scared happy confused wonderful magical

📝 Story Template (Optional):

When I traveled to: [Write past, future, or both]

I went there because: [Write why you wanted to travel]

What I saw: [Write about the things you saw]

Who I met: [Write about people or creatures you met]

What happened: [Write about your adventures]

How I felt: [Write about your feelings]

What I learned: [Write about what you learned]

Would I go again: [Write yes or no and why]

What year or time period?
