Writing Worksheet - Week 3

Write a Weather Report

Instructions:

Write a weather report like a real weather person! Tell about today's weather, the temperature, what the sky looks like, and what the weather might be like tomorrow. You can look outside or use your imagination!

Weather Report:

Today's Weather Report - Be a weather reporter and tell everyone about the weather!

☀️ Sunny

Clear blue sky

☁️ Cloudy

Gray clouds

🌧️ Rainy

Water falling

⛈️ Stormy

Thunder and lightning

❄️ Snowy

White snow falling

💨 Windy

Strong wind blowing

Weather Report Ideas:

🌡️ Temperature Report

Tell how hot or cold it is today

☁️ Sky Conditions

Describe what the sky looks like

🌬️ Wind Report

Tell if it's windy, calm, or breezy

🌧️ Precipitation

Tell if it's raining, snowing, or dry

👕 What to Wear

Suggest what clothes people should wear

🔮 Tomorrow's Forecast

Predict what the weather might be like tomorrow

Weather Report Template:

Today's Date: [Write today's date]

Current Temperature: [How hot or cold is it?]

Sky Conditions: [What does the sky look like?]

Wind: [Is it windy or calm?]

Precipitation: [Is it raining, snowing, or dry?]

What to Wear: [What clothes should people wear?]

Tomorrow's Forecast: [What might the weather be like tomorrow?]

Temperature Words:

Hot: boiling, scorching, warm, toasty

Cold: freezing, chilly, cool, cold

Just Right: mild, pleasant, comfortable, nice

Weather Report Tips:

  • Start with "Good morning/afternoon, this is your weather report"
  • Include the current temperature
  • Describe what the sky looks like
  • Tell about wind, rain, or other conditions
  • Suggest what clothes to wear
  • End with a forecast for tomorrow
  • Use weather words from the word bank
