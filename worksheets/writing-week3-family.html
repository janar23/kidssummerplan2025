Writing Worksheet - Week 3

Write About Your Family

Instructions:

Write about your family! Tell about the people in your family, what they like to do, and what makes your family special. You can write about family members, family activities, or what you love about your family.

Writing Prompt:

All About My Family - Write about the people in your family and what makes them special!

Writing Ideas (Choose one or write about whatever you want!):

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 My Family Members

Write about each person in your family - their names, what they look like, and what they like to do

🏠 Family Activities

Write about fun things your family does together - games, meals, trips, or special traditions

💝 What I Love About My Family

Write about what makes your family special and why you love them

🎉 Family Celebrations

Write about birthdays, holidays, or other special times with your family

🍽️ Family Meals

Write about eating together, favorite foods, or cooking with your family

🏖️ Family Adventures

Write about trips, outings, or fun activities you do with your family

Family Writing Template:

My Family: [Write about who is in your family]

What we like to do: [Write about family activities]

What makes us special: [Write about what you love about your family]

My favorite family memory: [Write about a special time together]

Writing Tips:

  • Start with "My family is..." or "I love my family because..."
  • Include names of family members
  • Describe what each person looks like or likes to do
  • Write about activities you do together
  • Share special memories or traditions
  • Tell why your family is important to you
