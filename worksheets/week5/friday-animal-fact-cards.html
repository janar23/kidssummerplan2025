📋 What to Include in Your Fact Cards

🔍 Fact Categories:

  • Animal Name: What is this animal called?
  • Habitat: Where does this animal live?
  • Diet: What does this animal eat?
  • Size: How big is this animal?
  • Special Features: What makes this animal unique?
  • Fun Fact: What's something amazing about this animal?
  • Conservation Status: Is this animal endangered?

🐾 Animal Facts to Help You

🌍 Amazing Animal Facts:

  • Lions: Live in groups called prides, can roar very loudly
  • Elephants: Largest land animals, have amazing memories
  • Dolphins: Super smart, can communicate with each other
  • Penguins: Can't fly but are excellent swimmers
  • Giraffes: Tallest animals, have long necks to reach leaves
  • Tigers: Largest cats, have unique stripe patterns
  • Pandas: Love bamboo, have black and white fur
  • Eagles: Have amazing eyesight, can see from very far away
  • Sharks: Have been around for millions of years
  • Kangaroos: Hop around, carry babies in pouches
  • Butterflies: Start as caterpillars, taste with their feet

🔍 Research Tips

How to find information about your animals:

  • ✅ Ask your family about animals they know
  • ✅ Look up information in books or online (with help)
  • ✅ Watch nature documentaries
  • ✅ Visit a zoo or aquarium
  • ✅ Use your imagination for creative facts
  • ✅ Draw pictures of your animals

🌟 Presentation Ideas

How to share your fact cards:

  • 📖 Read your cards to family members
  • 🎨 Add drawings or pictures to your cards
  • 📱 Take photos of your cards to share
  • 🎭 Act out the animals while presenting
  • 🎵 Make up a song about your animals
  • 🏆 Have a family quiz about your animals
