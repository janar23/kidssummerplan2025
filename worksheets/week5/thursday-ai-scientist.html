🤖 What is AI?

🧠 Artificial Intelligence Facts:

  • AI: Computers that can think and learn like humans
  • Machine Learning: AI that gets smarter by practicing
  • Robots: Physical machines that can move and do tasks
  • Voice Assistants: AI that can talk and answer questions
  • Self-Driving Cars: AI that can drive without a human
  • Medical AI: AI that helps doctors find diseases
  • Game AI: AI that plays games with you
  • Creative AI: AI that can draw, write, and make music

💼 AI Scientist Jobs

🔬 Different Types of AI Scientists:

  • Research Scientist: Creates new AI technologies
  • Data Scientist: Teaches AI using lots of information
  • Robotics Engineer: Builds AI robots
  • AI Programmer: Writes code for AI systems
  • AI Teacher: Trains AI to understand and learn
  • AI Doctor: Creates AI for healthcare
  • AI Artist: Makes AI that creates art
  • AI Explorer: Sends AI to space or deep ocean
