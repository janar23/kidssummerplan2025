📝 Recipe Writing Tips

💡 How to Write a Great Recipe:

  • Be specific: Use exact measurements (cups, tablespoons, etc.)
  • List everything: Don't forget any ingredients!
  • Step by step: Write instructions in order
  • Be clear: Use simple words that everyone can understand
  • Add details: Include cooking time, temperature, and special tips
