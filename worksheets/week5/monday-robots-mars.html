Week 5 - Monday Writing Worksheet

Today's Mission: Write about robots helping on Mars!

🌟 Your Mission Briefing

Imagine you're a scientist on Earth, and you've just received a message from robots working on Mars!

Write a story about how robots are helping humans explore and learn about the Red Planet. What are they doing? What problems are they solving? How do they communicate with Earth?

🤖 Robot Facts to Help Your Story

Real Mars Robots:

  • Perseverance Rover: Collects rock samples and looks for signs of ancient life
  • Curiosity Rover: Studies Mars' climate and geology
  • Ingenuity Helicopter: First aircraft to fly on another planet
  • Opportunity Rover: Explored Mars for 15 years (now retired)

What Robots Do on Mars:

  • Take pictures and send them back to Earth
  • Drill into rocks to study what's inside
  • Measure temperature, wind, and radiation
  • Look for water and signs of life
  • Test new technologies for future missions

✍️ Your Story Planning

Before you write, think about:

  • What is your robot's name?
  • What special job does it have on Mars?
  • What exciting discovery does it make?
  • How does it help scientists on Earth?
  • What challenges does it face?
